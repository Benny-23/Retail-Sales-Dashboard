# 📊 Retail Sales Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyzes retail sales performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The dashboard was built in Power BI to provide business insights into sales, profitability, customer segments, and regional performance.

---

## Dashboard Preview

![Dashboard](images/Dashboard.png)

---

## Business Problem

Retail managers needed an interactive dashboard to monitor sales performance across regions, categories, and customer segments while identifying trends and opportunities to improve profitability.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales and profit
- Track monthly sales trends
- Compare regional performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Analyze profit by sub-category
- Enable interactive filtering

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

---

## KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin

---

## Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top Products
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Interactive Slicers

---

## Key Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales.
- Consumer customers represented the largest sales segment.
- High discounts reduced profitability for some product categories.
- The West region contributed the highest sales.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Reduce excessive discounts on low-margin products.
- Increase inventory for top-performing products.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-profit regions.
